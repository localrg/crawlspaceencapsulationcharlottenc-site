Catawba Crawlspace Co. uses this website to provide crawlspace encapsulation information, receive homeowner inquiries, and respond to service requests in Charlotte, Gaston County, Mecklenburg County, and nearby North Carolina communities.

When a visitor submits a contact form, the website may collect the visitor's name, phone number, email address, city, project notes, and any other information the visitor chooses to provide. The website may also receive basic technical information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, referral source, date, time, and general website usage details through hosting logs or analytics tools.

Catawba Crawlspace Co. uses submitted information to respond to crawlspace review requests, communicate with homeowners, schedule conversations, prepare relevant service recommendations, improve website content, maintain website security, and keep appropriate business records. The company may use general usage information to understand which service pages are helpful to local visitors.

Contact Forms and Web3Forms

This website uses Web3Forms to process form submissions. When a visitor submits a form, the information entered into that form is transmitted through Web3Forms so Catawba Crawlspace Co. can receive and respond to the inquiry. Catawba Crawlspace Co. does not sell personal information. The company may share information with service providers that help operate the website, deliver form submissions, support communications, maintain business systems, comply with legal obligations, protect rights and safety, or complete a business transaction if ownership or operations change.

The website may use basic cookies, hosting analytics, or similar technologies to understand website performance and visitor activity. A visitor can use browser settings to block or delete cookies, although some website features may not work the same after those settings are changed.

Catawba Crawlspace Co. keeps information for as long as needed to respond to requests, provide service, maintain records, improve operations, and meet legal or business requirements. Visitor Choices

A visitor may contact Catawba Crawlspace Co. to request an update, correction, or deletion of information submitted through this website. The company may need to keep certain information when required for legal, security, accounting, dispute-resolution, or business-record purposes.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for homeowners and adults seeking crawlspace encapsulation information. The website is not intended for children, and Catawba Crawlspace Co. does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
