Article focus

Where wood rot risk shows up

Inspectors look at floor joists, beams, sill plates, subflooring, pier contact points, plumbing penetrations, duct surroundings, and areas below bathrooms, kitchens, and shaded exterior walls. Soft wood, staining, fungal growth, or insect activity should be documented.

02

Why encapsulation helps

A vapor barrier, sealed vents, drainage planning, and humidity control reduce the exposure that keeps wood damp. That helps protect the floor system after any required repair or cleaning work is complete.

03

What homeowners should compare

A strong proposal should separate moisture control from structural repair. It should explain what will be sealed, what should remain visible, how humidity will be managed, and which wood conditions require a qualified repair recommendation.
