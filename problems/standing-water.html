Article focus

Where standing water comes from

Water may enter through poor grading, short downspouts, foundation-wall seepage, plumbing leaks, low crawl space spots, blocked drain paths, or stormwater that collects around the foundation after heavy rain.

02

Why covering it is not enough

A vapor barrier can hide damp soil, but it should not trap unresolved water. Active water can lift liner edges, damage insulation, stress wood framing, create odor, and make the crawl space hard to maintain.

03

How to plan the fix

The project should review drainage, sump pump placement, waterproofing support, liner routes, dehumidifier drains, and access paths so the finished crawl space can handle Charlotte rain patterns without turning the liner into a cover for a recurring problem.
