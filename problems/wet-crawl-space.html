Article focus

Common signs

Homeowners may notice damp soil, musty odors, insulation that falls or feels heavy, condensation on ducts, staining on masonry walls, pest activity, soft wood, or air in the home that feels humid even when the HVAC system is running.

02

What should be checked first

The inspection should separate vapor from water. Exposed soil and open vents point toward encapsulation and air control, while standing water, downspout discharge, low crawl space areas, and foundation seepage may require drainage planning before the liner is installed.

03

How encapsulation helps

A complete system covers the soil, seals seams and vents, addresses wall edges, reviews drainage, and adds dehumidifier-ready humidity control when needed. The result is a crawl space that is easier to monitor after Charlotte rain and humidity cycles.
