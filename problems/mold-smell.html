Article focus

Why the smell travels upward

Crawl space air can move through gaps around ducts, plumbing, wiring, subfloor seams, and floor penetrations. If the crawl space stays humid, that air can carry musty odors toward the living space above.

02

What to do before encapsulation

Visible growth, stained wood, wet insulation, or strong odor should be evaluated before the system is sealed. Encapsulation controls the moisture conditions that allow growth, but mold remediation or cleaning may be needed before the crawl space is finished.

03

How the system reduces future risk

The moisture-control plan should combine a vapor barrier, air sealing, vent closure, drainage review, dehumidifier planning, and maintenance checks so the crawl space does not keep returning to the same humid condition.
