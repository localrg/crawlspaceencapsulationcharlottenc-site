Main idea

What sagging floors can signal

Uneven floors, soft spots, bouncy rooms, cracks above openings, or doors that no longer close cleanly may connect to joists, beams, subflooring, supports, piers, or moisture-damaged wood below the home.

02

Why moisture matters

High humidity, condensation, standing water, and poor ventilation can raise wood moisture and create conditions for decay or pest activity. Before installing a liner, the inspection should identify any repair, support, or wood rot concerns.

03

How encapsulation fits the repair path

After structural conditions are understood, encapsulation can help reduce the moisture exposure that contributes to future deterioration. The finished crawl space should keep beams, piers, access routes, and utilities visible for maintenance.
