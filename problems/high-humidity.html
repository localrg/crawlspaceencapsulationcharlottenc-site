Why humidity stays high

Warm outdoor air can enter through foundation vents and gaps, then contact cooler surfaces below the floor. Exposed soil, damp insulation, leaky ducts, poor drainage, and shaded lots can keep relative humidity elevated for long stretches.

02

What high humidity affects

Sustained humidity can contribute to musty odors, condensation, mold growth conditions, wood moisture, insulation failure, pest activity, and HVAC efficiency problems. The issue is often felt upstairs before the homeowner sees it below.

03

How to control it

The system should combine ground vapor control, vent sealing, air leak reduction, drainage review, dehumidifier planning, and periodic maintenance checks. A sealed crawl space is easier to keep within a practical humidity range.
