Main idea

Why water comes first

A liner can control vapor, but it should not be used to hide active water problems. Standing water, wet soil, foundation seepage, clogged drains, or poor exterior grading can continue to stress the crawl space after encapsulation if the water path is not addressed.

02

Drainage details to review

The inspection checks outside grade, gutter discharge, downspout extensions, foundation wall stains, low areas in the crawl space floor, sump pump placement, drain routes, access limitations, and how water would move during heavy Charlotte rain events.

03

How drainage connects to encapsulation

Once the water path is planned, the vapor barrier, wall liner, pier wrapping, access door, dehumidifier drain, and finished maintenance path can be designed around a crawl space that is cleaner and easier to monitor.
