Article focus

The sources of crawl space moisture

Charlotte crawl spaces can collect moisture from exposed soil, humid outdoor air, foundation wall seepage, poor grading, disconnected downspouts, standing water, condensation on ducts, and air leaks around penetrations. Each source needs a different control point.

02

How encapsulation controls moisture

The system uses a durable vapor barrier, sealed seams, closed vents, wall treatment, pier wrapping, access improvements, drainage planning, and dehumidifier placement when needed. These pieces work together so the crawl space becomes easier to inspect and less exposed to seasonal humidity swings.

03

Why moisture control supports the rest of the home

A controlled crawl space can support indoor air quality, energy efficiency, floor-system durability, duct performance, utility access, and fewer conditions that favor mold growth, pests, wood rot, and musty odors.
